FwKnop-ng is an Android client to open firewall ports and wake machines remotely. It supports <a href="https://www.cipherdyne.org/fwknop/">fwknop</a> (Single Packet Authorization), classic port knocking and Wake-on-LAN.

With fwknop, it sends an encrypted, authenticated SPA packet that makes a fwknopd server open a firewall port for your IP address. With classic port knocking, it sends a configurable sequence of ports with a delay between packets. With Wake-on-LAN, it wakes up a machine on your network.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li>Three modes: fwknop SPA, classic port knocking and Wake-on-LAN</li>
<li>Rijndael (AES) and GPG (via OpenKeychain) encryption, HMAC authentication</li>
<li>Configurations stored encrypted (Android Keystore, Tink, SQLCipher)</li>
<li>Optional app lock (biometrics or device credential)</li>
<li>Encrypted backup and restore of all configurations</li>
<li>Import/export of configurations through QR codes</li>
<li>NFC tag triggered knocks</li>
</ul>

<b>Note:</b> configurations are bound to the device's Android Keystore. Removing the screen lock or a factory reset makes them unrecoverable: keep your fwknop keys elsewhere, or use the encrypted backup.
