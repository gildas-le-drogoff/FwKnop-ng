FwKnop-ng is an Android client for <a href="https://www.cipherdyne.org/fwknop/">fwknop</a> (Single Packet Authorization). It sends an encrypted, authenticated SPA packet that makes a fwknopd server open a firewall port for your IP address. It also handles classic port knocking (knockd style) and Wake-on-LAN.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li>Rijndael (AES) and GPG (via OpenKeychain) encryption, HMAC authentication</li>
<li>Classic port knocking: knockd-style sequence of TCP/UDP ports, with an adjustable delay</li>
<li>Wake-on-LAN on the local network or through a router, optional SecureOn password</li>
<li>Configurations stored encrypted (Android Keystore, Tink, SQLCipher)</li>
<li>Optional app lock (biometrics or device credential)</li>
<li>Encrypted backup and restore of all configurations</li>
<li>Import/export of configurations through QR codes</li>
<li>Knocks and Wake-on-LAN triggered by an NFC tag</li>
</ul>

<b>Note:</b> configurations are bound to the device's Android Keystore. Removing the screen lock or a factory reset makes them unrecoverable: keep your fwknop keys elsewhere, or use the encrypted backup.
